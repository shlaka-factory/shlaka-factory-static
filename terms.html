1. What Shlaka Factory does

Shlaka Factory is a desktop tool that helps short-form video creators produce 9:16 vertical videos on their own computer and upload the finished video to the creator's own TikTok account(s). The TikTok integration uses TikTok Login Kit for authentication. When the creator approves the finished video, the app calls the Content Posting API Direct Post endpoint (/v2/post/publish/video/init/) with the creator-chosen caption, privacy_level, and engagement flags. The video posts directly to the creator's TikTok feed at the creator-selected privacy level.

The same operator runs two content-vertical TikTok handles — @red_team_analysis and @redteamanalysis — under a single legal entity; the second handle is reserved for a separate content vertical. These Terms apply identically to use of Shlaka Factory with either account.

Content compliance. Because Direct Post bypasses the in-app finalize step, the creator confirms on each upload that the video, caption, and all included material comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines and all applicable laws before clicking upload.
