1. The app

Shlaka Factory is an in-house tool that produces vertical 9:16 short-form videos and publishes them to our own authorized TikTok account. It is operated by our own team and is not offered to the public as a hosted service.

2. How it works

The tool authenticates the account owner through TikTok Login Kit and publishes finished videos through the TikTok Content Posting API. Every video is produced and reviewed by us before publishing; the tool publishes only to accounts we own and operate, at the privacy level we select, and applies the AI-generated-content label where applicable. Posts are attributed to Shlaka Factory, per TikTok's guidelines.
