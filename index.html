What it is

Shlaka Factory is the in-house production and publishing tool behind our media brand Red Team Analysis (TikTok @red_team_analysis). We create original short-form educational videos — sleep science, health, and myth-busting explainers in a vertical 9:16 format — and use Shlaka Factory to publish them to our own authorized TikTok account.

How our TikTok integration works

Shlaka Factory authenticates the account owner through TikTok Login Kit (OAuth) and publishes finished videos through the Content Posting API.

  • Operator-reviewed. Every video is produced and reviewed by us before it is published. Nothing is posted without that review.
  • Own accounts only. We publish exclusively to TikTok accounts we own and operate — never on behalf of any third party.
  • Two posting paths. Upload sends the video to the account's drafts for final review in the TikTok app; Direct Post publishes the reviewed video to the account at the privacy level we select.
  • Honest labeling. AI-assisted videos are published with TikTok's AI-generated-content label (is_aigc).
  • Disclosed. Posts made through the app are attributed to Shlaka Factory, per TikTok's guidelines.

Permissions we request

  • user.info.basic — confirm which account we are publishing to (open ID, display name, avatar).
  • video.upload — send a finished video to the account's drafts for review.
  • video.publish — publish a reviewed video directly to our own authorized account.

We do not request access to existing videos, followers, messages, analytics, or any other user's data.

Data & privacy

Shlaka Factory runs locally on our own machine. The OAuth token TikTok issues is stored in a local file on that machine and is never sent to any external server. Videos upload directly from our machine to TikTok. We do not sell data and do not share TikTok data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy.
