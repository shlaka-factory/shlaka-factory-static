Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-15

1. Who this policy is for

Shlaka Factory is an in-house tool used by our own team to publish our own original short-form videos to our own authorized TikTok account. It runs locally on our machine. This policy explains what TikTok data the tool touches and how we handle it.

2. Data we access from TikTok

Through TikTok's APIs we request only:

  • user.info.basic — the account's open ID, display name, and avatar, to confirm which account we are publishing to.
  • video.upload — to send a finished video to the account's drafts for review.
  • video.publish — to publish a reviewed video directly to our own authorized account.

3. Data we do NOT access

The tool does not request or access existing videos, follower lists, direct messages, comments, analytics, or any information about other TikTok users.

4. Where data is stored

The OAuth tokens TikTok issues are stored in a local file on our own machine. They are never transmitted to, or stored on, any external or third-party server.

5. Where the video goes

Videos upload directly from our machine to TikTok's Content Posting API endpoint. There is no intermediate storage or third-party processor.

6. AI-generated content

Where a video is produced with AI assistance, it is published with TikTok's AI-generated-content label (is_aigc), in line with TikTok's transparency requirements.

7. Third parties

Shlaka Factory does not share TikTok data with any third party, does not sell data, and runs no advertising or analytics on TikTok data.

8. Children

The tool is not directed to anyone under 13 (or the minimum age required by TikTok in your region) and is used only by our own team.

9. Data retention and deletion

Access can be ended at any time by deleting the local token file or by revoking the app under TikTok → Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services.

10. Changes to this policy

Material changes are reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

11. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because processing is minimal and local, the lawful bases are consent and legitimate interest. Where applicable, you retain rights of access, rectification, and erasure. Requests: adasha97816@gmail.com.
