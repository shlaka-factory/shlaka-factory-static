Privacy Policy — Shlaka Factory

Last updated: 2026-05-17. App name: Shlaka Factory. Contact: adasha97816@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy describes how Shlaka Factory (the "app", "we", "our") handles data when a creator uses the app to produce short-form vertical videos on their own machine and post them to their own TikTok account(s). The same operator runs two content-vertical TikTok handles — @red_team_analysis and @redteamanalysis — under a single legal entity; this policy applies identically to both.

1. Who this policy is for

Shlaka Factory is a desktop tool used by short-form video creators working in educational niches (personal finance, career advice, applied science, productivity). The app runs locally on the creator's own computer. This policy applies to any creator who connects their TikTok account to Shlaka Factory.

2. Data Shlaka Factory accesses from TikTok

When a creator connects their TikTok account to Shlaka Factory, the app requests two TikTok API permissions:

  • user.info.basic — used to fetch the creator's open_id, display_name, and avatar_url. Shlaka Factory shows the display name and avatar in its connection panel ("Connected as @handle") so the creator can verify they are about to publish to the correct account before clicking upload.
  • video.publish — used so that when the creator explicitly clicks the upload button, Shlaka Factory can publish the finished mp4 file directly to the creator's TikTok account via the Content Posting API Direct Post endpoint (/v2/post/publish/video/init/), using the creator-chosen caption and privacy level supplied in the same UI.

3. Data Shlaka Factory does NOT access

Shlaka Factory does not request and does not access:

  • The creator's existing videos on TikTok.
  • The creator's followers, follows, or any social graph data.
  • The creator's direct messages, comments, or notifications.
  • Any analytics or insights on the creator's TikTok account.
  • Any TikTok user other than the connected creator.

4. Where data is stored

Shlaka Factory runs entirely on the creator's own machine. The authentication tokens TikTok issues during OAuth are stored in a local JSON file (.tiktok_tokens.json) on the creator's machine. This file is never transmitted to any third-party server operated by us. The creator's display name and avatar shown in the app are read from TikTok at session start and held only in process memory during the session.

Tokens persist on the local machine only and expire on TikTok's schedule (typically 24 hours for access tokens, 365 days for refresh tokens). We operate no server-side credential store: there is no cloud database, no backup of tokens, and no remote copy of any creator's OAuth material.

5. Where the video goes

When the creator clicks upload, the video file is uploaded directly from the creator's machine to TikTok's Content Posting API endpoint. Shlaka Factory does not host, proxy, or store the video on any intermediate server. The upload uses the Direct Post flow: the app calls /v2/post/publish/video/init/ with the creator-supplied caption and privacy_level, and TikTok publishes the video directly to the creator's feed.

6. Third parties

Shlaka Factory does not share TikTok user data with any third party. Shlaka Factory does not sell, rent, or trade any data. The app does not embed advertising SDKs and does not run analytics on TikTok content.

7. Children

Shlaka Factory is not directed to children under 13. Creators using the app must be of the minimum age required by TikTok's own Terms of Service in their jurisdiction.

8. Data retention and deletion

A creator can disconnect their TikTok account at any time by deleting the local .tiktok_tokens.json file or by revoking Shlaka Factory inside the TikTok app under Settings → Security and login → Manage apps. Once revoked, the app can no longer fetch user info or upload videos until the creator goes through the OAuth consent flow again.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of Shlaka Factory after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Your rights under GDPR and CCPA

Shlaka Factory processes a minimal amount of personal data (open_id, display_name, avatar_url, and OAuth tokens) on the creator's own machine. The lawful bases under the EU/UK GDPR are (a) the creator's explicit consent given during the TikTok OAuth flow and (b) our legitimate interest in operating the OAuth handshake that connects the app to TikTok.

EU, EEA, and UK creators have the rights set out in GDPR Articles 15 to 17 — access, rectification, and erasure — as well as Article 20 portability. Because no data leaves the creator's machine, access is exercised by reading the local .tiktok_tokens.json file and the in-app connection panel, and erasure is exercised by deleting that file and revoking the app inside TikTok (see Section 8).

California residents: under the CCPA, we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. EU/EEA/UK data subject and CCPA requests can be sent to adasha97816@gmail.com.
